About

I am interested in how organizations make better decisions: how they define priorities, allocate resources, measure progress, and turn fragmented information into meaningful action. My work sits at the intersection of business analytics, strategy, operations, and technology, with a focus on building systems that make organizations more effective and informed.

I am a Statistics major with an emphasis in Data Science at San Diego State University, minoring in Leadership Development. With 2+ years of undergraduate experience across workforce analytics, public health surveillance, scientific research, and institutional operations, I have developed a technical foundation grounded in a broader organizational perspective: understanding not only what the data says, but how it can improve decisions and execution.

My professional experience has spanned several environments. At SDSU Career Services, I led workforce analytics supporting a $2M California State University initiative, reconstructing fragmented datasets spanning 761K+ records into standardized analytical pipelines. Today, at SDSU's Global Education Office, I support institutional analytics and data governance, developing reporting infrastructure and improving consistency across enterprise systems to strengthen operational visibility and decision-making.

Alongside my institutional work, I have been developing independent AI-enabled analytics products. My current project, Twenty AI CRM Copilot, connects operational CRM data with locally hosted language models to transform business records into structured insights, KPI interpretations, and executive-ready reporting. I am exploring agent-based distribution and potential commercialization, extending my interests beyond analytical workflows into independent product development.

My research experience includes developing statistical models for a $1.27M NSF student success program, building validation workflows for international SARS-CoV-2 surveillance data at the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, and engineering Python and SQL pipelines processing 3.5 TB of astronomical time-series data through the NSF-DOE Vera C. Rubin Observatory research program. Across these environments, I have learned to approach complex problems through reproducible analysis, reliable data infrastructure, and measurable outcomes.

I also founded SDSU's first Data Science Initiative, building a campus-wide community that grew to 160+ members and supported more than 20 students in securing internships and professional opportunities. That experience shaped how I think about leadership: not simply as individual achievement, but as the ability to build durable systems, develop others, and leave organizations stronger than I found them.

My contributions to student leadership and service have been recognized through the 2025 Daniel B. Nowak Outstanding Student Service Award, the 2026 Prebys Student Leaders Endowed Scholarship, and SDSU's 2026 Leadership & Service Homecoming Royal recognition. These experiences reinforce a perspective I carry into my technical work: meaningful impact comes from combining analytical rigor with responsibility for the people and organizations those systems serve.

Long term, I am preparing for a career in business analytics, strategy and operations, or product-oriented roles where I can combine statistical thinking, technical execution, and people-centered leadership. I am particularly interested in the growing intersection of AI and organizational decision-making: building systems that move beyond reporting to help people understand problems, evaluate opportunities, and act with greater confidence.
