This is not a homework archive. It is a structured personal knowledge system designed to preserve what I learned, demonstrate how I applied it, and make the connections between academic study and real-world experience visible.

How the archive is organized

The repository is organized around my two academic areas of study and the leadership experiences that extended beyond the classroom. Its structure emphasizes meaningful documentation rather than collecting every assignment or course artifact.

Statistics & Data Science

  • Lower Division: Foundational coursework, summarized syllabi, and selected learning materials that established my technical and quantitative foundation.
  • Upper Division: Coursework, notes, examples, applied work, and reflections connected to statistics, computing, modeling, and data science.

Leadership Development Minor

  • Lower Division: Foundational leadership coursework and documentation of core concepts.
  • Upper Division: Selected course artifacts and reflections emphasizing leadership development, organizational thinking, and professional growth.

Extracurricular Leadership

Documents leadership experiences outside the classroom, including organizations I founded or served in leadership roles for. The emphasis is on applied leadership, organizational development, decision-making, and contributions rather than participation alone.

Academic integrity & documentation

This repository intentionally excludes graded examinations, homework submissions, and copyrighted instructional materials. It is intended to document personal learning and provide professional context, not replace coursework or redistribute academic resources.

Substantive content and artifacts reflect my own academic work and understanding. AI tools were used sparingly and transparently for limited supporting tasks, such as syllabus summarization, rather than as a substitute for original reasoning or academic work.

A record of continuous development

The purpose of maintaining this archive is to make learning cumulative. Instead of treating each semester as an isolated experience, I wanted a system that would help me revisit concepts, connect ideas across disciplines, and carry useful lessons into future work.

This archive provides the academic and developmental context behind the projects and experiences featured throughout this portfolio. My primary case studies focus on workforce analytics, strategy and operations, AI-assisted reporting, and large-scale data science.

It is more than a record of the classes I completed. It is a record of how I learned, what I chose to pursue, and how those experiences shaped the professional direction I am building toward.
