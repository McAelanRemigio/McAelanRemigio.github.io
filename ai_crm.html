Twenty AI CRM Copilot

An AI-powered CRM intelligence platform that transforms operational customer data into structured business insights, executive summaries, and decision-ready reporting through locally hosted language models.

Built around Twenty CRM, PostgreSQL, and Ollama, the project explores how AI agents can extend existing business systems by interpreting operational data, explaining performance signals, and turning CRM activity into actionable intelligence.

The broader vision is to make AI-assisted business intelligence accessible through reusable workflows and agent-based integrations, with potential applications beyond a standalone CRM reporting tool.

The problem

CRM platforms capture valuable operational information: customer interactions, company records, activities, pipeline changes, and relationship histories. However, collecting information and understanding it are two different challenges.

Teams often rely on manual reviews, dashboards, and fragmented reporting processes to understand what is happening across their customer and operational data. Even when the information is available, translating it into meaningful business insights requires additional analytical work.

The opportunity is to introduce an intelligence layer that works with existing CRM infrastructure, using structured data and AI to make business information easier to interpret and act upon.

The solution

Twenty AI CRM Copilot connects CRM records to a database-backed analytical workflow and locally hosted language models. Rather than asking an LLM to independently interpret an entire database, the system uses structured information and defined workflows to provide context for AI-generated outputs.

The result is a foundation for automated business reporting, contextual summaries, KPI interpretation, and operational intelligence that can be integrated into existing workflows.

Product philosophy

The goal isn't to replace business analysts or decision-makers with AI. It's to reduce the distance between operational data and useful understanding.

Reliable data structures provide the foundation. Business logic establishes context. Language models make information easier to interpret and communicate. Human judgment remains central to the decisions that follow.

Beyond the prototype

An important direction for this project is making its capabilities accessible beyond a standalone application.

AI agent ecosystems introduce another distribution model: instead of requiring users to operate a separate analytics application, specialized capabilities can be packaged into reusable skills that agents invoke as part of larger workflows.

I am exploring this direction alongside potential marketplace distribution and commercialization opportunities. The broader objective is to develop practical AI tools that solve recognizable business problems and can eventually reach users beyond their original development environment.

Why this matters

Businesses have invested heavily in systems that collect and organize operational data. The next opportunity is making that information substantially more useful through AI.

Twenty AI CRM Copilot explores that opportunity by bringing together data infrastructure, business intelligence, and locally hosted language models in a practical application.

For me, this project represents an intersection of technical development and product thinking: identifying an operational problem, engineering a solution, exploring new distribution models, and investigating how independent software can create measurable business value.
